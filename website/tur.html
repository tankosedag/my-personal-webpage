Cities I Visited in Türkiye

Ankara: Ankara is where I live and study, so it has an important place in my everyday life. In my free time, I enjoy exploring different parts of the city and spending time with my friends.

İstanbul: My friends and I travelled to Istanbul to visit a friend and spend time together. The city's lively atmosphere made it a memorable setting for our trip, while being together made the experience even more enjoyable.

Samsun: During my childhood, I visited Samsun with my family on sightseeing and cultural trips. These visits gave me the chance to discover new places and remain part of my early memories of travelling with my family.

Ayvalık: I visit my grandparents in Ayvalık every summer, making it a place I feel closely connected to.

Sındırgı: I completed my first internship at a mine in Sındırgı, Balıkesir. It gave me the opportunity to apply what I had learned at university and gain practical experience, making it an important step in my professional development.

Antalya: With its Mediterranean coastline, historical surroundings and natural beauty, Antalya offers a variety of places to explore. It brings together the relaxed atmosphere of a coastal city and opportunities for cultural sightseeing.

Mersin: I spent a summer holiday with my friends in Mersin. Taking a break from our everyday routines and spending time together made this trip a memorable part of my summer.

İzmir: I have visited İzmir several times and enjoyed its vibrant atmosphere and beautiful coastline. Its connection to the sea gives the city a distinctive character and makes it a pleasant place to explore.

Bodrum: My aunt and her family live in Bodrum and I used to visit them every year when I was little.

Göynük: Göynük is my mother's hometown, and I have visited it a few times.

Bursa: My friends and I stopped in Bursa during our journey to Istanbul, it was more of a culinary tour..

Çanakkale: During my childhood, when I was involved in sports, I attended camps in Çanakkale more than once. These visits were part of an active period of my life, and I associate the city with my childhood sports experiences.

Çorum: I visited Çorum on a school trip focused on history and ancient civilisations. Exploring the region with my classmates gave me the opportunity to learn beyond the classroom and made it a memorable educational trip.
